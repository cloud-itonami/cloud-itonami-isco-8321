# Business Model: Independent Motorcycle Courier Dispatch Practice

## Classification

- Repository: `cloud-itonami-isco-8321`
- ISCO-08: `8321`
- Occupation: Motorcycle Drivers
- Social impact: local-jobs, road-safety, transport-access

## Customer

- courier/delivery riders (gig or employed)
- local businesses and e-commerce senders
- delivery-dependent customers

## Offer

- delivery/trip dispatch scheduling
- rider-roster and route-assignment coordination
- vehicle maintenance-order coordination
- safety-concern intake and escalation

## Revenue

- per-delivery dispatch fee
- monthly dispatch-platform fee
- maintenance-coordination service fee

## Trust Controls

- no route/traffic-navigation decision is ever finalized by this system, and a rider's on-road safety judgment is never overridden — a permanent, non-overridable block
- a rider/license record must be independently verified and registered before this system may reference that rider in any proposal
- maintenance orders above the registered cost ceiling always require human sign-off
- safety concerns (vehicle defect / road hazard / rider fatigue) always escalate to a human, never auto-resolved
- dispatch and maintenance records are auditable, not editable
